### How would you like to use vllm on ascend

I am deploying the Qwen3-Embedding-0.6B model using vllm serve. I noticed that when using different versions of vLLM to serve the exact same model with identical inputs, the output embedding vectors are slightly different, resulting in a cosine similarity of less than 1.0.
Since embedding models should be deterministic, I would like to understand the root cause of this discrepancy and find a reliable way to fix/pin the unique vector values
