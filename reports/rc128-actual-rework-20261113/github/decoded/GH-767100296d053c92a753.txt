[Performance]: Qwen3-235B accuracy and performance issues on releases/v0.26.0rc
## Model
Qwen3-235B-A22B-w8a8-rot and Qwen3-235B W4A8 (800IA3)

## Issue
On `releases/v0.26.0rc`, some PD separation and PD disaggregation tests show lower accuracy or performance than the baseline.

For the A3 single-machine aclgraph case (`32768_4096_TPOT50ms`), the measured TPOT values are `258.2517, 260.6926, 263.8598, 255.5208`, compared with the baseline `273.432`.

This issue will be tracked in the follow-up sunset plan.
